🛒 MG Buy MG Lategame Helper · 0.19.1

Add or share codes

Paste one or more links or codes (from the game on a PC: Settings → 📱 Phone remote → "Copy phone link", or someone's "Buy-only link"). Opening such a link adds them too. Codes stay on this phone: they're after the # in the link, which isn't sent to the website.

The Buy-only link lets whoever opens it press Buy on these PCs (only on the ones whose owner switched sharing on) and see what's to buy there; it can't be turned into your pairing codes.

The full link is for your own devices only: it can also Reconnect your PCs.

This page presses Buy everything on a PC, with the same list and checks as pressing Buy there; it never connects to the game itself, so nothing gets disconnected. Messages go through public MQTT relay servers (HiveMQ and EMQX), encrypted with keys made from the codes.
